The Bolt: EV Adoption Analysis

Analyzed Washington State electric vehicle registration data to identify high-opportunity locations for EV charging infrastructure. Built a data model and analytical framework to reveal geographic adoption trends, assess infrastructure readiness, and deliver strategic recommendations for a fictional EV startup — Bolt.

Skills: SQL · Data Modeling · Geographic Analysis · Strategic Consulting

Quant Decision Analysis

Conducted quantitative feasibility studies for two business cases: Wagner Fabricating Company's make-vs-buy production decision, and River City Fire Department's resource optimization problem. Applied cost analysis, inventory modeling, safety stock calculations, and break-even analysis to deliver data-backed recommendations.

Skills: Cost Modeling · Inventory Analysis · Decision Analysis · Operations Research
